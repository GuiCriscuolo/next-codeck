## Critical rules

### Historical Timeline
Use slides 41–48 only for chronological historical evolution: company history, product evolution, acquisitions, software versions, professional trajectory or other time-based milestones.

Do **not** use Historical Timeline for processes, methodologies, customer journeys, operational flows or sequences of activities.

### Process Roadmap
Use slide 57 for a conceptual or operational sequence: process, methodology, customer journey or workflow. The sequence does not need to represent calendar dates.

### Product Roadmap
Use slide 58 for future product planning, releases, features or initiatives distributed by quarter or period.

### Flexible Content
Slides 5 and 54–59 use the flexible/custom composition approach. Preserve the master identity, title system, grid, footer and visual language while creating the required composition.

### Layout selection
Select by communication purpose first, content volume second.
